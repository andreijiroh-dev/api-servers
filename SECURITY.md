# Security policy

Please email `security@andreijiroh.dev` if you see any security issue in the
codebase or the services themselves. DO NOT file them publicly in the issue tracker or submit patches unless cleared
and coordinated privately.

Regarding the use of gen AI tools for patches, even through it's a personal 
project of mine, you must disclose them when submitting reports and patches
privately if you do and may ask a copy of those transcripts as part of reviewing those.
